About Me

I also spent time studying abroad in Japan, which gave me the chance to improve my Japanese and experience a completely different environment. Learning another language has made me think a lot more about communication, design, and how people interact with information.

Japanese Study Companion

One project idea I'm interested in is a study tool for learning Japanese. The idea is to combine readings, vocabulary, grammar explanations, and flashcards into one place.

I want it to focus on making Japanese study material easier to work through instead of constantly switching between different resources.

Ideas for the project

  • Toggleable furigana
  • Vocabulary definitions
  • Grammar explanations
  • Flashcards

D2L Assignment Dashboard

I'm working on an assignment dashboard concept that makes it easier to see upcoming work across classes without checking each course separately.

The goal is to make due dates and assignment details easier to scan, especially during busy weeks.
